Paid Ads Sprint

Six weeks to find out which ad actually sells the thing.

A structured test of audiences, hooks and landing copy across Meta and Google, run properly with a clean measurement setup, so at the end you know what to spend money on.

Who this is for

Anyone who has boosted a post, seen nothing happen, and wants it done with a method.

What you get

Five deliverables, each with a name and a date. Nothing on this list is conditional.

  • 01Pixel, conversion and GA4 setup checked and fixed before a cent is spent.
  • 02Three audience sets against three creative angles — nine combinations, tested in sequence.
  • 03A rewrite of the landing page headline, subhead and call to action to match the winning angle.
  • 04Weekly one-page report: spend, cost per result, and the decision taken that week.
  • 05A closing document naming the winning combination and the budget to scale it at.

How this one runs

You pay the link on this page. Within a few minutes you get a receipt and a short intake form — usually six or seven questions. The moment that comes back, the clock starts on the turnaround quoted at the top of this page, and you get a single point of contact who is also the person doing the work.

If we miss the date for a reason that is ours, you are not billed for the overrun and we say so before you have to ask.

Questions about Paid Ads Sprint

Does the price include ad spend?

No — spend is paid directly to Meta and Google from your own account so you keep the data and the history. Budget from $20 a day is enough to learn something.

What if nothing works?

Then you have saved yourself a year of guessing. The closing document says so plainly and recommends where to look instead.

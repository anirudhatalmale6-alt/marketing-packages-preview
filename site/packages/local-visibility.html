Local Visibility

Be the business that comes up when someone nearby searches for what you sell.

Google Business Profile built out properly, local listings made consistent, review flow set up, and the on-page work that makes the map pack notice you.

Who this is for

Trades, clinics, salons, restaurants — anyone whose customers are within driving distance.

What you get

Five deliverables, each with a name and a date. Nothing on this list is conditional.

  • 01Google Business Profile claimed, categorised, described, and filled with photos and services.
  • 02Twenty directory listings created or corrected so your name, address and phone match everywhere.
  • 03A review request flow — QR card, SMS wording and a short follow-up sequence.
  • 04One optimised local landing page with schema markup and embedded map.
  • 05A ranking snapshot before and 30 days after, so the change is visible.

How this one runs

You pay the link on this page. Within a few minutes you get a receipt and a short intake form — usually six or seven questions. The moment that comes back, the clock starts on the 14 working days turnaround quoted above, and you get a single point of contact who is also the person doing the work.

If we miss the date for a reason that is ours, you are not billed for the overrun and we say so before you have to ask.

Questions about Local Visibility

Will I rank number one?

Nobody can promise a position. What this does is remove every reason Google has to overlook you, which is the part that is actually in our control.

Do you need access to my Google account?

Yes, as a manager on the Business Profile. You stay the owner and can remove access at any time.

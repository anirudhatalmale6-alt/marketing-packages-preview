Questions

The things people ask before paying

If something is not answered here, send it over before you buy — we would rather turn work down than take money for the wrong package.

How do I pay?

Every package page has a direct payment link. You click it, pay by card on a secure hosted checkout, and we are notified immediately. No invoices to chase, no cart to fight with.

What happens straight after I pay?

You get a confirmation receipt and a short intake form within a few minutes. Work starts as soon as that comes back — usually the same working day.

Can I combine packages?

Yes, and most clients do. Pay for them separately or ask for a combined link if you would rather make one payment.

Are these prices final?

The listed price is what you pay. If something about your business genuinely sits outside the scope, we say so before taking money, not after.

Do you work with my industry?

Almost certainly. The packages are built around how buying decisions get made, which varies far less between industries than people expect.

Can I get a refund?

If we have not started, yes, in full. Once work is underway we would rather fix the thing you are unhappy with — that has always worked out better for both sides.
